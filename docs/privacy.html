Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Calendar-On-Demand ("we", "our", or "us") is a desktop application designed to display and manage your Google Calendar events directly on your Windows desktop. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy.

2. Data We Access

To provide its core functionality, Calendar-On-Demand requests access to your Google Calendar data (specifically, the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar scope). This allows the application to read your upcoming events to display them on your desktop and write new events when you choose to schedule them through the app.

3. How We Use and Store Your Data

Crucially, Calendar-On-Demand is a local application. All data retrieved from your Google Calendar is processed and stored locally on your device. We do not operate any external backend servers, and we never transmit, upload, sell, or share your calendar data or personal information with ourselves or any third parties.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

Calendar-On-Demand's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Revoking Access

You can revoke Calendar-On-Demand's access to your Google account at any time by visiting your Google Account's Security settings page and removing access for this application. You can also clear all local data by using the sign-out feature within the application itself.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our GitHub repository.

macOS Installation Guide
Follow these quick steps to get Calendar-On-Demand running smoothly on your Mac.
1 Drag to Applications

Open the downloaded .dmg file, and drag the Calendar-On-Demand icon into your Applications folder.

2 One-time Gatekeeper Bypass

Because this app is free and not signed through Apple's paid developer program ($99/year), macOS will block it on first launch. To unlock safely, open Terminal (⌘ + Space → type "Terminal") and run:

xattr -cr /Applications/Calendar-On-Demand.app
3 Ready to Launch!

Launch Calendar-On-Demand from Spotlight (⌘ + Space) or your Applications folder. When updating to new releases in the future, simply replace the app and re-run the command once.
